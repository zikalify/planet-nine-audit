What this project is

This project independently re-checks the published Planet Nine evidence: the orbital-clustering statistics, the survey exclusions, and the unconfirmed infrared candidates. Everything runs on public data and public code, and every claim links to its source. Corrections are noted beside the claims they change.

Nothing here is peer reviewed, and no observed position of Planet Nine exists. The evidence map shows one published model's potential locations, not a detection.
