What this project is

This project independently re-checks the published Planet Nine evidence: the orbital-clustering statistics, the survey exclusions, and the unconfirmed infrared candidates. Everything runs on public data and public code, and every claim links to its source. Wrong turns and corrections stay in the public record.

Nothing here is peer reviewed, and no observed position of Planet Nine exists. The evidence map shows one published model's potential locations, not a detection. Analysis and writing are by Paul Hill and his AI agent.
