What changes between the two toy models?

The narrow model assigns weights 3:1 to particles A:B. The broad model uses 1:3. Archive A detection probabilities are 0.8 and 0.1; archive B uses 0.7 and 0.2. North follow-up uses 0.9 and 0.1; south uses 0.1 and 0.9. There are no real sky coordinates behind these names.

Within a shared group, union coverage lies between the maximum probability and the sum capped at one. Independent groups combine through their miss probabilities. The Python implementation includes input validation, derivation and tests.

03 / Research context

Methods exist. The evidence is unsettled.

Keep alternative models visible

A 2026 conditional-likelihood study reports 1.9-sigma clustering evidence for its 25-object sample and warns earlier search maps may be too narrow. This depends on stated modeling assumptions, and does not settle whether a planet exists.

Siraj, Chyba & Tremaine (2026) ↗

Candidates need follow-up

The IRAS/AKARI candidate pair does not determine a full orbit. It cannot justify a unique present-day marker. This prototype does not confirm, fit or propagate that candidate.

IRAS/AKARI search (2025) ↗

Build beside established tools

Sorcha models survey selection. KBMOD searches images for moving sources. Planet Nein already combines Planet Nine populations and Sorcha. This small auditor is not a replacement or a proven new scientific method.
