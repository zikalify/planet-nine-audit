Audit log: re-checking the raw IRAS scans behind one reported Planet Nine candidate

27 October 2026: The numerical scan-fit findings previously on this page have been withdrawn after a fitting-method flaw was identified. The analysis is being rerun; the previous numerical results should not be used or cited.

Context: in 2025, Phan et al. reported a Planet Nine candidate - a far-infrared source in the 1983 IRAS survey (IRAS F02211-4844) paired with a detection 23 years later in AKARI data. This project is re-examining the underlying 1983 scan data, obtained from IRSA's SCANPI service, rather than the published co-added image.
